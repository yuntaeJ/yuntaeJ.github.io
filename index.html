Research

Civil Engineering & Architecture

  • Site representation and progress evaluation. My research combines neural radiance fields (NeRF) with building information modeling (BIM) to represent construction scenes and evaluate progress.
  • Construction safety. My work explores worker motion prediction across multiple cameras to support construction safety.

AI & Computer Vision

  • 3D scene understanding and reconstruction. My research focuses on accurate 3D object segmentation and language-driven scene interpretation using vision-language models.
  • Robust detection and tracking. My research explores low-light image enhancement for fisheye object detection, object recognition models that are robust to domain shifts, and multi-camera synchronization for cross-camera object tracking.
